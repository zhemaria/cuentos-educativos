The story

At the neighborhood park, Lucía's favorite swing broke. The chain hung down with no seat. "Who can fix it?" Lucía asked. "The city ," Grandpa answered.

"The government is the group of people who lead a city, a state, or a country," Grandpa explained. "It makes rules and provides public services."

Grandpa told her that Chicago has a , the person who leads the city. It also has alderpersons. An represents the neighbors in one part of the city and takes their problems to the government.

"What else does the government do?" asked Israel, who was playing in the sand. Grandpa pointed all around. "It takes care of parks, picks up trash, fixes streets, and pays firefighters." That is called a : work the government does for everyone.

The government also makes laws. A is a rule that everyone must follow. For example, one law says that cars must stop at a stop sign. That keeps people safe when they cross the street.

That afternoon, Lucía and Israel wrote a letter to the neighborhood alderperson. "The swing in the park is broken. Many children want to use it. Can you fix it, please?" They signed it with their names.

Two weeks later, the swing had a new seat! City workers had fixed it.

"You helped your ," said Grandpa. "A community is a group of people who live in the same place and take care of one another." Lucía got on the swing and flew as high as she could.

Glossary

government
The group of people who lead a city, a state, or a country. It makes rules and provides public services.
mayor
The person who leads the government of a city.
alderperson
In Chicago, the person who represents the neighbors in one part of the city and takes their problems to the government.
public service
Work the government does for all people, like picking up trash or taking care of parks.
law
A rule that all people must follow.
community
A group of people who live in the same place and take care of one another.
