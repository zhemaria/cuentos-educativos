Glossary

save
To keep money instead of spending it, so you can use it later.
bank
A business that keeps people's money safe and lends money.
savings account
A place at the bank where a person's money is kept and the amount is written down.
interest
Extra money the bank pays you for keeping your money there, or that you pay when you borrow money.
loan
Money or something valuable that someone gives you now and that you promise to pay back later.
pay back
To give money back to the person you borrowed it from.
debt
Money a person still has to pay back.
