The story

At recess, Ms. Ríos's class had only one soccer ball. Everyone wanted to play with it. "We have a problem of ," said the teacher. "There is scarcity when there is not enough of something for everyone who wants it."

On Monday, Israel grabbed the ball first and kept it for the whole recess. He played with his two friends, and the other children had to watch from the bench. That was his : what he decided to do.

Israel had a lot of fun. But when they went back to the classroom, he saw that Lucía and other classmates were sad. His choice had a good for him and a bad consequence for the others. A consequence is what happens because of something we did.

That afternoon, Lucía's grandpa picked up both children at school. "I did not like seeing my friends sad," Israel told him. "Our choices affect us and they affect others too," Grandpa answered. "Sometimes in a good way, and sometimes in a bad way."

The next day, the teacher gathered the class. "The ball is a for everyone," she explained. "A resource is something we use to do what we need or want. How can we use it better?"

The children shared ideas and reached an : each group would play with the ball for a while. Israel suggested using the playground clock to time the : ten minutes for each group.

Since then, everyone plays at recess. While they wait for their turn, they jump rope or play hopscotch. "Now my choice makes everyone happy, and I have fun too!" said Israel.

Lucía smiled. She had learned that, before making a choice, it is good to ask: "Who will be affected by what I do?"

Glossary

scarcity
When there is not enough of something for everyone who wants it.
choice
What a person decides to do among several options.
consequence
What happens because of something we did. It can be good or bad.
resource
Something we use to do what we need or want, like a ball, water, or time.
agreement
A decision that several people make together after talking.
turn
The time when it is each person's or group's chance to do something, one after another.
