The story

Mrs. Rosa’s cat had four kittens.

Génesis and Adrián went to meet them.

The mother cat has black and white fur.

Two kittens are black and white, like their mother.

One kitten is gray, like its father, the neighbor’s cat.

And the littlest kitten is white, with a black spot on her nose.

“They are all alike in some ways,” said Génesis. “They have whiskers, tails and pointy ears, like their parents.”

“But each one is a little different,” said Adrián.

Adrián held up his green dinosaur, and the little kitten sniffed its tail.

“Your dinosaur doesn’t look like anyone here!” Génesis laughed.

Mrs. Rosa told them the kittens will grow up to be big cats.

Young animals are like their parents, but they are not exactly the same.
