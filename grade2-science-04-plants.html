The story

At Lucía and Israel's school, Ms. Ríos brought three flowerpots. In each one there was a little bean plant of the same size. "We are going to do an ," she said. "We want to find out if plants need light and water to grow."

The teacher gave each pot a letter. Plant A would be by the window, with and water. Plant B would be by the window, but with no water. Plant C would get water, but it would live inside a dark closet.

"We change only one thing for each plant," the teacher explained. "That way we will know what causes each result." Every day, the children observed the plants and drew what they saw.

After two weeks, the three plants looked very different. Plant A had grown tall and strong. Its was firm and its leaves were green.

"What can we make?" the teacher asked. Israel raised his hand. "Plants need light and water to grow healthy!"

"Exactly!" said Ms. Ríos. "Now let's take care of plants B and C so they can get better." Lucía watered plant B, and Israel carried plant C to the window.

Glossary

investigation
A way to find the answer to a question by doing tests and observing carefully.
sunlight
The light that comes from the Sun. Plants use it to make their food.
stem
The part of a plant that holds up the leaves and flowers and carries water from the roots.
wilt
When a plant becomes dry, droopy, and weak, for example when it has no water.
root
The part of a plant that grows under the soil. It holds the plant in place and takes in water.
conclusion
What we learn at the end of an investigation, after we look at the results.
