Glossary

canyon
A very deep valley with tall rock walls that a river forms little by little.
erosion
The slow wearing away of rock and soil by water or wind.
surface of the Earth
The outside part of our planet, where we find soil, rocks, and water.
volcano
A mountain where melted rock comes out from inside the Earth.
lava
Very hot melted rock that comes out of a volcano. When it cools, it becomes hard rock.
earthquake
A strong, fast shaking of the ground that lasts a few seconds.
