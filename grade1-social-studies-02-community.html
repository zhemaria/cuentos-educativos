The story

The neighborhood park was full of trash and dry leaves.

The mayor could not clean it all by herself.

So the neighbors decided to help on Saturday.

Israel’s mom brought bags and gloves.

A man fixed the broken swing.

Israel and Adrián picked up cans to recycle.

Some girls painted the bench green.

Nobody paid them: they were all volunteers.

At the end, the park was clean and pretty.

“We did it together!” Adrián shouted, holding his dinosaur up high.

“It’s not only the leaders who help,” said Israel’s mom. “We are all part of the community.”

Every person can do something important for the community.
