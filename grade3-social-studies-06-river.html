The story

Glossary

environment
Everything around people: land, water, air, plants and animals.
modify
To change something. People modify the environment when they build canals, dams or roads.
adapt
To change the way you live so you do well in a place, like bundling up in winter.
canal
A waterway made by people to carry water or to let boats pass.
pollute
To make water, air or land dirty with trash or harmful substances.
natural resource
Something from nature that people use, like water, wood or soil.
climate
The usual weather of a place over many years.
