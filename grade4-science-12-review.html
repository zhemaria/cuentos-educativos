The story

To end the year, there was a science night in the library. Its lamps ran partly on solar panels on the roof, because sunlight is a renewable resource. Ms. Patel hid a treasure chest and set up seven clues, one for each friend.

The first clue led Israel to a ramp with a blue toy car. Israel let it go from the very top. "The faster the car goes, the more it has," he said, "and when it crashes, it passes energy to whatever it hits." The car knocked over a box hiding the second clue.

Daniel found a battery, some wires and a bulb that wasn't lit. He connected the loose wire, and the bulb lit up and felt a little warm. "The has to be closed for the current to flow," he explained. "And the bulb converts electrical energy into light and heat." There was another clue taped to the bottom of the bulb.

Sophia shook a jump rope hard. "If I shake it with more energy, the gets taller. It has more amplitude. And if I shake it faster, the wavelength gets shorter." Selena counted five crests, so the next clue was at table five.

At table five, Salma found a message of dots and dashes written backward, plus a mirror. She held the mirror up to the paper, and Athena decoded it with her Morse code alphabet. "We see things because light off them and goes into our eyes," she said. "And a mirror reflects almost all the light." The message said: "Find the turtle."

Athena flipped her sketchbook open to her drawing of the painted turtle. "Its shell is a that protects it," she remembered. "And its eyes and nose take in information that its brain processes to decide what to do." Under a stuffed turtle, they found a fossil and a note.

Elías held up the : a seashell pressed into stone. "A seashell fossil in a rock layer is evidence that the place was covered by water a very long time ago." The note said: "Follow the water."

Selena found a tray of sand with little channels in it and a watering can. She poured water, and the sand washed down to the end. "That's ," she said. "Water carries the soil somewhere else." "And in really heavy rain, we stay safe from floods," Israel added. Buried in the sand was a key.

The key opened the chest. Inside were seven "Grade 4 Scientist" medals and a note from Ms. Patel: "The real treasure is everything you know now." Israel looked at his sandy friends. "The treasure is great," he said, "but I still want the cookies on the next table."

Glossary

energy
The ability to make something move or change. It can pass from one object to another.
circuit
The complete path an electric current travels, from the battery and back again.
wave
A repeating movement that carries energy from one place to another.
reflect
When light hits something and bounces off.
structure
A part of a living thing that has a shape and a job.
fossil
The remains or prints of a living thing from long, long ago, saved in rock.
erosion
When water, wind or ice carry bits of rock and soil away to another place.
