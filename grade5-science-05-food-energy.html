The story

El cuento está en inglés. Pulsa «Listen to the story» para escucharlo y toca las palabras verdes cuando no entiendas una.

It was taco day in the cafeteria, and Selena was holding up her chicken taco like a trophy. "This is my favorite lunch," she said. "It gives me so much for recess."

"Do you know where that energy came from?" asked Athena, opening her sketchbook. "From the cafeteria kitchen," said Israel, with his mouth full. "Before that," said Athena. "Ms. Kowalski said that almost all the energy in our started in the sun."

Selena looked at her taco. "There's no sun in my taco. It's a tortilla, chicken and lettuce." Athena drew a big bright sun at the top of the page. "Let's follow the energy backward," she said. "Start with the tortilla."

"Tortillas are made from corn," said Sophia. "And corn is a . The corn plant used to make its own food, and it stored some of that energy in its kernels." Athena drew an arrow from the sun to a tall corn plant.

"And the chicken?" asked Selena. "Chickens can't use sunlight to make food." "No," said Athena, "but the chicken ate corn and seeds. So the chicken got its energy from plants, and the plants got it from the sun." She drew another arrow, from the corn plant to a chicken.

"The lettuce is easy," said Israel. "It's a plant too. It caught sunlight with its leaves." "So every part of my taco is full of energy from the sun," said Selena. "It's a sunshine taco."

Athena drew the last arrow, from the chicken to a girl running across the page. "And now you eat it," she said. "Your body uses that energy to move, like when you run at recess."

"That's not all," said Sophia. "Your body also uses the energy from food to keep its , even when it's cold outside, and to grow." Selena nodded. "And when I scraped my knee last week, my skin healed." "Right. The body uses food to itself, too," said Athena.

Selena looked at her drawing on the sketchbook page: sun, corn, chicken, girl. "So when I run at recess," she said slowly, "I'm really running with sunshine." "Exactly," said Athena. Israel stood up. "Then I'm going to go play soccer with my sunshine," he said, and he grabbed one more taco for the road.

Glossary

energy
The ability to make something move, grow, warm up or change.
energía: La capacidad de hacer que algo se mueva, crezca, se caliente o cambie.
sunlight
Light from the sun. It carries energy that plants can use.
luz del sol: La luz que viene del Sol. Lleva energía que las plantas pueden usar.
food
What living things eat. Food gives animals energy and material to grow.
alimento: Lo que comen los seres vivos. El alimento da a los animales energía y material para crecer.
plant
A living thing that uses sunlight to make its own food.
planta: Un ser vivo que usa la luz del sol para fabricar su propio alimento.
body warmth
The heat our body makes to stay warm. It uses energy from food.
calor del cuerpo: El calor que produce el cuerpo para mantenerse tibio. Usa energía de los alimentos.
repair
To fix. The body uses food to repair skin, muscles and bones.
reparar: Arreglar. El cuerpo usa los alimentos para reparar la piel, los músculos y los huesos.

Questions

Elige la respuesta correcta. Si te equivocas, el cuento te muestra dónde está la respuesta.

  1. AQuestion A. How did the corn plant get its energy?

    ¿Cómo obtuvo su energía la planta de maíz?

    The corn plant used sunlight to make its own food and stored some of that energy in its kernels.

  2. BQuestion B. Where did the chicken get its energy?

    ¿De dónde sacó su energía el pollo?

    The chicken ate corn and seeds, so it got its energy from plants, which got it from the sun.

  3. CQuestion C. What does Selena's body use the taco's energy for at recess?

    ¿Para qué usa el cuerpo de Selena la energía del taco en el recreo?

    Her body uses that energy to move, like when she runs at recess.

  4. DQuestion D. What else does the body use food energy for?

    ¿Para qué más usa el cuerpo la energía de los alimentos?

    The body uses food energy to keep its warmth and to grow.

  5. EQuestion E. How did Selena's scraped knee heal?

    ¿Cómo sanó la rodilla raspada de Selena?

    The body uses food to repair itself, so her skin healed.

Challenge: explain your thinking

Trace the energy

Choose your favorite breakfast. Draw arrows to show how its energy came from the sun to you. Then name two things your body does with that energy.

Sigue la energía. Elige tu desayuno favorito. Dibuja flechas para mostrar cómo su energía llegó del Sol hasta ti. Después nombra dos cosas que hace tu cuerpo con esa energía.

Answer in your notebook or talk it over with a classmate. Use evidence from the story.

Responde en tu cuaderno o coméntalo con un compañero. Usa evidencia del cuento.

Check your thinking

Example: sun → wheat plant → bread → me, and sun → grass → cow → milk → me. My body uses that energy to move, to stay warm, to grow and to repair itself. Every food path starts with a plant that used sunlight.
