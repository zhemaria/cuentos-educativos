"What about us?" asked Jamal. "Humans don't go through metamorphosis," laughed Mrs. Kim. "We are born as babies, grow into adults and look like our parents from the start."

At the end of the week, the class took the five butterflies to the school garden. One by one, they flew off to the flowers. Someday they would lay eggs, and the cycle would start again.

That afternoon, Israel drew a circle with arrows in his journal: egg, caterpillar, chrysalis, butterfly… and egg again. "That's why it's called a cycle," he thought. "It goes around and starts over."

Glossary

life cycle
The stages a living thing goes through: being born, growing, reproducing and dying. They repeat with each new generation.
be born
To start living, by hatching from an egg, coming from a mother or sprouting from a seed.
grow
To get bigger and change over time.
reproduce
To have young or make seeds, so there are new living things of the same kind.
larva
The young stage of some animals, which looks very different from the adult. A caterpillar is the larva of a butterfly.
chrysalis
The hard case in which a caterpillar changes into a butterfly.
metamorphosis
A big change in body shape during an animal's life, like from caterpillar to butterfly or from tadpole to frog.
