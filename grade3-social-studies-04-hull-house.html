The story

One Sunday, Great-Grandpa took Israel and Lucía to a big red brick house just west of downtown Chicago. "This is Hull-House," he said. "A story that changed the city started here."

More than 130 years ago, in 1889, a woman named Jane Addams opened this house with her friend Ellen Gates Starr. Back then, the was full of families who had just arrived from other countries.

Many families lived crowded into small rooms, worked long hours and had nowhere to leave their children or to learn English. The streets were dirty and there were few places to play.

Jane Addams wanted to things. At Hull-House she offered a kindergarten, English and art classes, a library, public baths and one of the first playgrounds in Chicago. Everything was open to the neighbors.

Jane Addams was also a : she worked to get new laws passed, like laws that protected children from working in factories and laws that improved garbage collection.

In 1931, Jane Addams won the Nobel Peace Prize. She was the first woman from the United States to win it.

Today, Hull-House is a . Lucía read a sign: "Thousands of people came every week." "It was like the heart of the neighborhood!" she said.

Great-Grandpa said that when he first came to Chicago, he took English classes at a community center like this one. "That's Jane Addams's ," he said: "the idea that neighbors can come together to offer and help those who need it most."

On the way home, Israel looked at the community center in his own neighborhood, where his mom helped on Saturdays. "People changed their community long ago," he thought, "and we can keep doing it."

Glossary

immigrant
A person who comes to live in a country different from where they were born.
neighborhood
A part of a city where neighbors share streets, stores and schools.
improve
To make something better than it was.
reformer
A person who works to change laws or customs that are unfair.
service
Work or help offered to people, like a class or a library.
museum
A place where important objects are kept and shown so people can learn about the past.
legacy
What a person leaves for the future that keeps helping after they are gone.
