The story

Génesis and Adrián are playing with a red ball in the hallway.

Adrián puts the ball on the floor, but it hardly moves.

"Let's make a ramp!" says Génesis.

They put a board on top of a book.

Adrián lets go of the ball at the top of the ramp. The ball rolls down!

"What if the ramp is higher?" asks Adrián. They use three books.

Now the ball rolls down faster and goes farther, all the way to the door!

Now they want the ball to go into the toy box, off to one side.

Génesis puts a block in the way. The ball bumps it, changes direction and rolls into the box.

"Our design works!" they both shout.

To know if it worked, they tried it many times and watched how far the ball went.
