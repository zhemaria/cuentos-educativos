The story

Pelusa, Génesis’s little dog, came back from the field covered in little balls.

They were burrs, seeds that stick to fur.

Génesis and Lucía looked at a burr with a magnifying glass.

It had lots and lots of tiny hooks!

“That’s why they hold on to Pelusa’s fur,” said Lucía.

Grandma told them that a man noticed the same thing a long time ago.

He copied the tiny hooks on the burrs and invented the Velcro on our shoes.

The girls wanted to copy nature too.

They thought about a turtle’s hard shell, which protects it.

They made a design: a hard helmet to protect your head on a bike.

Pelusa wagged her tail, with no more burrs.

Plants and animals give us ideas for new inventions.
