The story

When Israel went to visit Alejandro in Texas, Luna the dog had just had three puppies. The cousins sat on the grass to watch them.

"They look like their mom," said Israel. "They have golden fur, floppy ears and long tails." Those are the puppies from their : their mother and father.

A characteristic that is passed down is called an inherited . "Like your blue eyes," Israel told Alejandro. "You inherited them from your family."

But the puppies weren't exactly alike. One was lighter, one had a white spot on its chest and one was a little bigger. "Even though they are brothers and sisters, each one is different. That's called ," explained Alejandro's aunt.

On the patio table were two pots of beans that had been planted on the same day with the same kind of seeds. One was in the sun: it was tall and green. The other had ended up in the shade: it was thin and pale.

"The seeds were the same," said Alejandro, "but the changed them: light, water and the place where they grow also affect how they turn out."

Just then, Adrián rode by on his bike with training wheels, very proud. "Is riding a bike inherited too?" he asked. The cousins laughed.

"No," said Israel. "Riding a bike, reading or swimming aren't inherited: you them with practice." A scar on your knee isn't inherited either: it comes from what happens to you.

That night, Alejandro made a chart in his notebook with two columns: "I inherited it" and "I learned it or it happened to me." In the first he wrote: blue eyes, light brown hair. In the second: riding a bike, swimming and a scrape on my elbow.

Glossary

trait
A characteristic of a living thing. Inherited traits are passed from parents to their young.
inherit
To get characteristics from your parents, like eye color or the shape of your ears.
parents
The mother and father of a living thing.
variation
The differences between living things of the same kind, even between brothers and sisters.
environment
Everything around a living thing: light, water, food and the place where it lives.
learn
To come to know how to do something by studying or practicing.
characteristic
Something that sets a living thing or an object apart, like its color, size or shape.
