The story

After school, Israel ate a cookie on the sidewalk. A crumb fell, and in a few minutes a line of ants came to get it.

"Look!" said Lucía, who had a magnifying glass. "The crumb is bigger than an ant, but together they can carry it."

Ants live in a huge called a . Each ant has a job: some look for food, some take care of the eggs and some guard the anthill.

"Ants know how to ," Lucía explained. "They work together to do something one ant couldn't do alone." That's why they find so much food.

Just then, some geese flew by in a V shape. Israel remembered what he had read: when geese fly in a group, they take turns in front, so they get less tired on long trips.

That night, Israel watched a show about wolves with his family. Wolves live in a and hunt together. A single wolf couldn't catch a big animal, but a pack can.

Then a of silver fish appeared, swimming close together. When a big fish came, the school moved like one shiny cloud. That makes it harder to catch any one fish, so they all themselves.

"Why do so many animals live in groups?" asked Israel. "Because it helps them ," said his dad. "They find food, defend themselves and take care of their young."

The next day, Israel and Lucía made a poster for the class: "Together is better." They drew ants, geese, wolves and fish… and in one corner, all of their third grade classmates.

Glossary

group
Several living things that live or work together.
colony
A very large group of animals of the same kind that live together, like ants or bees.
cooperate
To work together to get something done.
pack
A group of animals, like wolves, that live, travel and hunt together.
school
A large group of fish that swim together.
survive
To stay alive in spite of dangers.
protect
To keep safe from danger or harm.
