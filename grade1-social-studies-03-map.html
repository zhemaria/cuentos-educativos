The story

The teacher hid a treasure in the classroom and gave them a map.

The map was a drawing of the classroom seen from above, the way a bird would see it.

Each table was a square, and the rug was a circle. They were symbols.

In one corner was the map key: it told what each symbol meant.

A red X marked the treasure.

Génesis found the door on the map. “We start here!”

Following the arrow, they walked toward the window.

Adrián turned when they got to the bookshelf.

Next to the plant, under the rug, there was the X!

The treasure was a box of star stickers.

Later, each child drew a map of their bedroom with their own map key.

A map helps us know where things are and how to get to a place.
