Snow in Chicago, sun in Texas

The story

In January, Israel and Alejandro had a video call. Israel was wearing a hat and a scarf. Alejandro was wearing a short-sleeved t-shirt!

"It's snowing here," said Israel. "It's sunny here," answered Alejandro. They were talking about the : what the air is like in a place at a certain time, whether it is cold or hot, rainy or sunny.

The cousins decided to do an experiment for school. Every morning for a month, they would measure the with a thermometer and write down whether there was any : rain or snow.

They recorded their in a table: the numbers and observations they collected. At the end of the month, Mrs. Kim helped them draw a bar , with a blue bar for Chicago and an orange bar for Texas.

The graph showed a pattern right away: all of the Texas bars were taller than the Chicago bars. In January, Texas was warmer almost every day.

"Is it always like that?" asked Israel. The teacher showed them graphs from many years. "Weather changes from one day to the next, but is the usual weather of a place over many years."

"Chicago has cold, snowy winters and hot summers," she explained. "Southern Texas has a warmer climate: winters are mild and it almost never snows." In each of the year, the weather in each place follows its own pattern.

With their data, the cousins could make predictions: in July, they would both be hot; in January, Israel would need his coat again and Alejandro probably wouldn't.

To finish, they sent each other a gift: Israel mailed Alejandro a wool hat "just in case," and Alejandro mailed Israel a pair of sunglasses "for the Chicago summer."

Glossary

weather
What the air is like in a place at a certain time: cold or hot, rainy, snowy or windy.
temperature
How hot or cold something is. It is measured with a thermometer.
precipitation
Water that falls from the sky, like rain, snow or hail.
data
Numbers and observations we collect to learn something.
graph
A drawing that shows data with bars, lines or symbols so it is easy to compare.
climate
The usual weather of a place over many years.
season
One of the four parts of the year: spring, summer, fall and winter.
