The story

Ms. Ríos handed out colored clay. "Today we are going to make a ," she said. "A model is a small copy that shows what something big is like." "We are going to show the shapes of land and water."

Lucía and Israel worked together on a piece of cardboard. Israel made a out of brown clay. It was tall, with steep sides and a white snowy top. Next to it, he made a hill, which is lower and rounder.

Between the mountain and the hill there was a low space. "That is a !" said Lucía. "It is the low land between mountains or hills."

They made a river of blue clay run through the valley. A river is water that always flows in the same direction. The river went down the valley and reached a lake, which is still water with land all around it.

On the other side of the cardboard, Lucía pressed the green clay until it was flat. "This is a ," she explained. "It is wide, flat land with no mountains." Israel added little trees and a farm.

At the end, they covered the edge of the cardboard with dark blue. It was the , a huge body of salt water. In the middle of the ocean, they put a little piece of green land.

"And what is that?" the teacher asked. "An !" said Israel. "It is land with water all around it."

The teacher smiled. "Your model shows mountains, hills, valleys, plains, rivers, lakes, the ocean, and an island," she said. "That is what our planet is like: it has many shapes of land and water."

Glossary

model
A small copy that shows what something big is like, such as a place or a building.
mountain
A very high landform with steep sides. It is taller than a hill.
valley
The low land between mountains or hills. A river often runs through it.
plain
Wide, flat land with no mountains or hills.
ocean
A huge body of salt water that covers much of the Earth.
island
A piece of land with water all around it.
