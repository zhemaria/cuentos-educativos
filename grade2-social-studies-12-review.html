The story

At the end of the year, the neighborhood held a street party in front of the garden. Lucía and Israel prepared a surprise: a giant of the neighborhood that they made, with everything they had learned.

On the map, they drew the park with the fixed swing. Next to it they wrote: "The city helps us with services for everyone."

Then they drew the garden with its ramp. "The garden is because everyone can take part," Lucía explained to the neighbors. Mrs. Elena clapped from her wheelchair.

On Mr. Julio's bakery, they glued a cardboard coin, to remember that money makes trading easier than bartering. Next to it, they drew a piggy bank. "First I , and then I bought the ball!" said Israel.

They also marked the goods that are produced in the neighborhood, like bread and tomatoes, and the , like the library, the clinic, and the school.

In one corner, they put the compass rose and an arrow pointing east that said "Lake Michigan." In another corner, they glued a photo of Valeria and the coquí, to remember that there are communities very different from theirs.

Grandpa looked at the map quietly. "I watched this neighborhood change for fifty years," he said. "You see it through the eyes of a child, and I see it through the eyes of a grandpa. Those are two different , and both are important."

That night, the neighbors hung the map in the library. Underneath, Lucía and Israel wrote: "A neighborhood is made by its neighbors, when they decide, share, and work together." And at the party everyone danced, even the plena that Valeria had taught them by video.

Glossary

map
A drawing that shows what a place looks like from above.
government
The group of people who lead a city, a state, or a country, make rules, and give services to the people.
equitable
Fair for everyone: each person gets what they need to be able to take part.
save
To keep money now so you can use it later.
services
Work that one person does to help another, like teaching, healing, or lending books.
perspective
The way a person sees something, based on what they lived through and how they felt.
