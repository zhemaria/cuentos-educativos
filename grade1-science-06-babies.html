The story

Israel and Lucía went to the park pond with Lucía’s grandpa.

By the water there was a nest with eggs.

One day, five ducklings hatched from the eggs. They were the mother duck’s young.

The ducklings walked in a line behind their mother.

One duckling fell behind and started peeping very loudly: peep, peep!

The mother duck heard it and went back for it.

Then a dog came running over.

The mother duck spread her wings and quacked to protect her ducklings.

The dog went away, and the ducklings hid under their mother’s wings.

“Just like my mom takes care of me,” said Israel.

Grandpa smiled. “Animal parents take care of their children too.”

Young animals call for help, and their parents feed them, care for them and protect them.
