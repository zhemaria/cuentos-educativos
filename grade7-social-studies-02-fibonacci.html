Glossary

merchant
A person who buys and sells goods to earn a living.
mercader: Una persona que compra y vende mercancías para ganarse la vida.
Roman numerals
A way of writing numbers with letters, like X for 10 and C for 100. It has no zero and no place value.
números romanos: Una forma de escribir los números con letras, como X para 10 y C para 100. No tiene cero ni valor posicional.
place value
The value a digit has because of its position. In 505, the first 5 means 500 and the last 5 means 5.
valor posicional: El valor que tiene una cifra según su posición. En 505, el primer 5 vale 500 y el último 5 vale 5.
innovation
A new idea, method or invention that changes the way people do things.
innovación: Una idea, un método o un invento nuevo que cambia la manera en que la gente hace las cosas.
fraud
Cheating someone, for example by changing a number in a record to steal money.
fraude: Engañar a alguien, por ejemplo cambiando un número en un registro para robar dinero.
accounting
Keeping careful records of money that comes in and goes out.
contabilidad: Llevar registros cuidadosos del dinero que entra y que sale.

Check your thinking

Benefits: faster calculations, written accounting, easier to compare prices and calculate interest. Risks: numbers like 0 could be changed into 6 or 9 to commit fraud, and many people did not know the new system yet. A good answer chooses a side and might add a safety step, like writing important amounts in words too.

Go further

Think of an innovation from your own time, like phone payments. What benefits and risks does it bring, and how are people trying to reduce the risks?

Para ir más lejos. Piensa en una innovación de tu época, como pagar con el teléfono. ¿Qué beneficios y riesgos trae, y cómo intenta la gente reducir esos riesgos?

Check your thinking

Example: phone payments are fast and you don't need cash, but someone could steal your password or your phone. To reduce the risks, phones use fingerprints, codes and alerts. Like the bankers of Florence, people accept an innovation when they find ways to make it safe.
