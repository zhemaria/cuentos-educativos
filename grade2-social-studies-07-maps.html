The story

One Saturday, Grandpa gave Lucía and Israel a folded sheet of paper. "It is a treasure ," he said with a smile. "A map is a drawing that shows what a place looks like from above."

The map showed the neighborhood: the streets, the school, the garden, the park, and the lake in the distance. There were little pictures everywhere. "Those are ," Grandpa explained. "A symbol is a small picture that stands for something real."

In one corner of the map, there was a box. "That is the ," said Grandpa. "The map key tells what each symbol means." The tree was the park, the book was the library, the cross was the clinic, and a red X marked the treasure.

In the other corner, there was a star with letters. It was the , which shows the four : north, south, east, and west. "Lake Michigan is east of our neighborhood," Israel read.

The children set off with the map. They walked north, passed the library, and turned west at the clinic. Every little while, they checked the map to see where they were.

The X was at Mr. Julio's bakery. The treasure was a bag of sweet bread to share! Mr. Julio had helped Grandpa plan the surprise.

While they ate, Israel's mom showed them the neighborhood on her phone. "This is a ," she explained. "You see it on a screen, and you can zoom in or out with your fingers."

Lucía compared the two maps. Grandpa's map had drawings made by hand; the one on the phone showed photos taken from the sky. "Both of them help us find places," she said. "And both of them lead to sweet bread!"

Glossary

map
A drawing that shows what a place looks like from above.
symbol
A small picture that stands for something real, like a tree for a park.
map key
The box on a map that tells what each symbol means.
compass rose
The star on a map that shows where north, south, east, and west are.
cardinal directions
The four main directions: north, south, east, and west.
digital map
A map you see on a screen, like a phone or a computer.
