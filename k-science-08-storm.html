Ready for the storm

A story about a storm and about saving water and caring for the Earth.

  • What people tell us about what the weather is going to be like.

The story

In the morning, Adrián and Génesis watch the weather forecast on TV.

"This afternoon there will be a storm," says the weather lady.

So they put away the toys in the yard and close the windows.

In the afternoon, the sky gets dark. Boom! Thunder rumbles.

Adrián and Génesis are safe inside the house. They listen to the rain.

When the storm is over, a rainbow comes out.

Before bed, Adrián brushes his teeth and turns off the water.

"That way we don't waste water," he says.

Génesis puts the plastic bottles in the blue bin to recycle.

"We take care of the Earth when we save water and recycle," says Génesis.
