Mrs. Carmen's bakery

A story about what you need to know to make bread and to cut hair.

Three new words

  • Things that are made and can be bought, like bread.

  • Work someone does to help you, like cutting hair.

  • Something you do well because you learned and practiced it.

The story

Génesis and Lucía visited Mrs. Carmen’s bakery.

She gets up very early to make bread.

She knows how to measure flour, knead dough and use the oven without getting burned.

“I learned from my grandmother, and I practiced for many years,” she told them.

Making bread is her skill.

Bread is one of the goods: things that are made and bought.

Next door was Mr. Luis’s barbershop.

Mr. Luis cut a boy’s hair very carefully.

Cutting hair is a service: work that helps another person.

“To cut hair, you have to know how to use scissors and a comb,” said Mr. Luis.

Génesis bought sweet bread for her whole family.

To make goods and provide services, people learn and practice.
