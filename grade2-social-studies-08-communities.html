The story

Ms. Ríos's class got a letter from a school in Puerto Rico. It was written by a girl named Valeria, who lives in a town near El Yunque forest. "Let's write back to her!" said the teacher. "But first, let's learn the of each place."

A characteristic is something that makes a place special. Some have to do with the : the climate, the water, the plants, and the animals. Others have to do with the of its people.

Valeria wrote that in her town it is warm all year and it never snows. That is its : the usual weather in a place. In Chicago, on the other hand, summers are hot and winters are very cold, with lots of snow.

Valeria also wrote about El Yunque, a rainforest where the coquí lives, a tiny frog that sings at night: "co-kee, co-kee." There are no rainforests near Chicago, but there is Lake Michigan, so big that it looks like a sea.

Then the class read about culture. Culture is the way of life of a group of people: their language, their food, their music, and their celebrations. In Valeria's town, people speak Spanish, eat rice with pigeon peas, and dance to the rhythm of bomba and plena.

In Chicago, people speak many languages, because families from many countries came to live there. Near Lucía's home, there are Mexican bakeries, Polish restaurants, and Puerto Rican stores.

Lucía and Israel wrote the answer. They told Valeria about the snow, the lake, and their neighborhood's favorite : the summer parade. A tradition is a custom that a community repeats year after year.

At the end, the teacher asked them to the two places. "They are very different," said Israel, "but both have families, schools, and children who love to play." Lucía added a P.S. to the letter: "Will you teach me to dance plena someday?"

Glossary

characteristic
Something that makes a place or a thing special and different from others.
environment
Everything around a place: the air, the water, the soil, the plants, and the animals.
culture
The way of life of a group of people: their language, food, music, and celebrations.
climate
The usual weather in a place during the year: whether it is hot or cold, rainy or snowy.
tradition
A custom that a community repeats year after year.
compare
To look at two or more things to see how they are alike and how they are different.
