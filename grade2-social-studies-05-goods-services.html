The story

In the summer, Israel traveled with his mom to visit his cousin Tomás. Tomás lives in a small town in central Illinois, surrounded by green fields. It is a : a place with few houses, lots of open land, and farms.

Tomás's dad is a . He grows corn and soybeans in his fields. "My dad produces ," said Tomás. "Goods are things you can touch, buy, and use, like corn or soybeans."

The town also had . A service is work that one person does to help another, like the vet who took care of the cows or the mechanic who fixed the tractors.

When he came back to Chicago, Israel told Lucía all about it. "Chicago is an ," she explained. "It is a big city, with lots of buildings and lots of people."

The two children made a list of what is produced in their neighborhood. They found goods, like the bread from Mr. Julio's bakery and the tomatoes from the garden. They also found many services: the teacher who teaches, the doctor who helps sick people, the bus driver, and the barber who cuts hair.

Then they compared the two lists. In Tomás's town, many people work in the fields and produce food. In Chicago, there are more factories, more stores, and all kinds of services.

"So communities help each other," said Israel. "The corn from the town comes to Chicago in trucks." "And the machines and medicines made in the city go to the town," Lucía added. "Each community something the other one needs!"

That night, Israel ate an ear of corn with butter for dinner. He thought about his uncle's fields and smiled: maybe that corn came from there.

Glossary

rural community
A place with few houses, lots of open land, and farms.
farmer
A person who works the land to grow and harvest food.
goods
Things you can touch, buy, and use, like corn, bread, or a toy.
services
Work that one person does to help another, like teaching, healing, or fixing something.

A city, with lots of buildings, streets, and people.
produce
To make or grow something that other people can use.
