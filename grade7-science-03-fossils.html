On the field trip to the natural history museum, the class stopped in front of a long skeleton in a glass case. It had a pointed snout, flippers and eye sockets as big as dinner plates. "Is that a dinosaur?" asked Sophia. "Not quite," said Mr. Mensah. "It's an ichthyosaur, a reptile that swam in the sea about 200 million years ago. And the first famous one was found by a girl not much older than you."

He told them about Mary Anning. She lived in Lyme Regis, a town on the coast of England, and her family was very poor. In 1811, when Mary was 12, her brother Joseph found a strange skull in the cliffs. About a year later, Mary found and carefully dug out the rest of the skeleton. People in town said the Annings had found a monster.

"New layers form on top of old ones, so in layers that have not been disturbed, the deeper you dig, the older the rock and the fossils inside it." Daniel drew the wall in his notebook and numbered the layers from the bottom up.

Mary Anning spent her whole life hunting fossils in the cliffs, often in dangerous storms, because that was when the rock broke open. She found the first complete skeleton of another sea reptile, the plesiosaur, and many more. She taught herself so much that famous scientists came to Lyme Regis to learn from her. Glossary

fossil
The remains or traces of a living thing from long ago, preserved in rock.
fósil: Los restos o las huellas de un ser vivo de hace mucho tiempo, conservados en la roca.
sediment
Small pieces of sand, mud and rock that settle at the bottom of water and can slowly turn into rock.
sedimento: Pedacitos de arena, lodo y roca que se asientan en el fondo del agua y poco a poco pueden convertirse en roca.
layer
A flat band of rock. In layers that have not been disturbed, the lower ones are older than the ones above them.
capa: Una franja plana de roca. En las capas que no se han movido, las de abajo son más antiguas que las de arriba.
extinct
A kind of living thing is extinct when all of its members have died and there are none left anywhere.
extinto: Un tipo de ser vivo está extinto cuando han muerto todos sus miembros y no queda ninguno en ninguna parte.
paleontologist
A scientist who studies fossils to learn about life in the distant past.
paleontólogo: Un científico que estudia los fósiles para conocer la vida del pasado lejano.
fossil record
All the fossils that have been found, arranged in order of age. It shows how life has changed over time.
registro fósil: Todos los fósiles que se han encontrado, ordenados por edad. Muestra cómo ha cambiado la vida a lo largo del tiempo.

  1. AQuestion A. How did Mary Anning help find the first famous ichthyosaur?

    ¿Cómo ayudó Mary Anning a encontrar el primer ictiosaurio famoso?

    Her brother Joseph found the skull in 1811, and about a year later Mary dug out the rest of the skeleton.

  2. BQuestion B. How did the ichthyosaur become a fossil?

    ¿Cómo se convirtió el ictiosaurio en un fósil?

    It sank, sediment covered it, and over millions of years the sediment turned into rock and the bones into a fossil.

  3. CQuestion C. In undisturbed rock layers, where are the oldest fossils?

    En las capas de roca que no se han movido, ¿dónde están los fósiles más antiguos?

    New layers form on top of old ones, so the deeper the layer, the older the rock and its fossils.

  4. DQuestion D. How do we know that ichthyosaurs are extinct?

    ¿Cómo sabemos que los ictiosaurios están extintos?

    We find their fossils in old rock layers, never in the newest ones, and no living ichthyosaurs exist.

  5. EQuestion E. What does the fossil record show?

    ¿Qué muestra el registro fósil?

    All the fossils, in order by age, show that living things have changed and that many kinds are now gone.
