The story

On Saturday, Israel and Selena went over to Daniel's house. His dad was baking chocolate chip cookies, and the kitchen smelled amazing. Pepper, the family's black cat, was asleep on a chair, curled up like a cinnamon roll.

"Let's play a guessing game with a blindfold," Daniel suggested. They covered Selena's eyes and handed her something to taste. She scrunched up her face so much that everyone burst out laughing. "Lemon! That's a lemon!"

"How did you know if you couldn't see?" Israel asked. "With your ," said Daniel's dad. "Your tongue tasted something sour, and your nose smelled the lemon too. Your tongue and nose are . They take in information."

"But a tongue doesn't know the word lemon," Daniel said, scratching his head. "Exactly. carry the message from the tongue to the , and the brain it. It makes sense of the message and decides what to do." And Selena's brain decided to make that hilarious face.

All of a sudden, a strange smell filled the kitchen. "The cookies!" Daniel's dad yelled. He pulled the tray out of the oven; a few cookies were a little burnt. Daniel held his nose right away. His nose picked up the burnt smell, and his brain recognized it thanks to : he had smelled something like it before, the day the toast caught on fire.

Selena reached for a cookie. The second she touched the hot tray, she yanked her hand back, faster than she could even think "Ouch!" "Some responses are super fast," Daniel's dad explained, "and they protect us from danger." Selena blew on her fingers. Luckily, she wasn't burned.

Then Daniel's dad grabbed a can of cat food and popped it open. Click. In one second, Pepper leaped off the chair and came racing into the kitchen. "Pepper's ears heard the sound of the can," said Daniel. "Her brain remembered that sound means food, and she by running in."

"Animals have senses and brains too," said Daniel's dad. "Some can hear or smell much better than we can. Pepper can hear sounds so soft that we don't even notice them."

Israel picked up a cookie that wasn't burnt and studied it carefully. "So it's like a team. The senses take in information, the nerves carry it, the brain processes it and the body responds." Then he took a bite. "And right now my brain says these cookies are awesome."

Glossary

senses
The ways the body takes in information: sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch.
sense organ
A body part that takes in information, like the eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin.
nerves
Very thin threads in the body that carry messages between the senses and the brain.
brain
The organ inside your head that takes in information, makes sense of it and decides what to do.
process
To take in information, understand it and decide what to do with it.
respond
To do something because of what the brain decided, like move, talk or back away.
memory
The brain's ability to store what we experience so we can use it later.
