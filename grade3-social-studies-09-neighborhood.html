The neighborhood timeline

The story

For a school project, Israel and Lucía had to tell the of a Chicago neighborhood. They chose Pilsen, where Great-Grandpa lived when he first came to the city.

"Let's make a ," said Lucía, "a line where we put events in , from the oldest to the most recent."

Great-Grandpa took them for a walk around the neighborhood. "More than a hundred years ago, at the end of the nineteenth , families from Europe came here," he said. "Many came from the region of Bohemia, and they named the neighborhood after a city there: Plzeň, which sounds like 'Pilsen' in English."

Those families built brick houses, churches and stores. Many of them worked in the factories and on the river docks.

"Over time, many of those families moved to other neighborhoods," Great-Grandpa went on, "and families from Mexico and other Latin American countries arrived. I came to Chicago during that time." They opened bakeries, stores and restaurants.

On the walls of the neighborhood were huge, colorful murals: families, monarch butterflies, corn and musicians. "The murals show the of the people who live here," Great-Grandpa explained: "their customs, their music, their food and their history."

Israel and Lucía wrote each event on their timeline: first the families from Europe, then the factories, then the arrival of Latin American families, and finally the murals and festivals of today.

"A neighborhood is like a person," Lucía said when they finished. "It grows and changes, but it keeps its history. Every group that came left something behind." That made the neighborhood richer.

In class, everyone clapped for their timeline. Jamal suggested that next year they make one for Bronzeville, his grandmother's neighborhood. Mei wanted to do Chinatown. Every Chicago neighborhood had its own story!

Glossary

history
The study of what happened in the past, and the story of those events.
timeline
A line where events are placed in the order they happened.
chronological order
The order in which things happened in time, from oldest to most recent.
century
A period of one hundred years.
immigrant
A person who comes to live in a country different from where they were born.
culture
The customs, language, music, food and beliefs of a group of people.
change
What makes something different from how it was before.
