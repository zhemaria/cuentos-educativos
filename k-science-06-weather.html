The story

Every morning, Adrián and Génesis look out the window.

Then they stick a picture on the weather calendar.

On Monday the sky is blue. It is a sunny day, so they stick on a sun.

On Tuesday, drops fall from the sky. It is a rainy day, so they stick on a cloud with raindrops.

On Wednesday there are gray clouds, but no rain. They stick on a cloud.

On Thursday it is very cold, and white flakes fall.

It is a snowy day! Adrián and Génesis make a snowman.

On Friday the sun comes out again. They stick on another sun.

At the end of the week, they count the pictures.

"There were two sunny days! That happened the most," says Génesis.

When we look at the calendar, we know what the weather has been like.
