The story

Glossary

piece
One of the parts that make up an object.
take apart
To separate the pieces of an object, one by one.
build
To put pieces or materials together to make something new.

Something built from pieces joined together that hold it up, like a bridge, a house, or a tower.

What we see, count, or measure that shows something is true.
