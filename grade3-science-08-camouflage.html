Glossary

camouflage
Colors, spots or shapes that help a living thing blend in with the place where it lives.
variation
The differences between living things of the same kind.
advantage
Something that helps a living thing survive, find a mate or have young.
predator
An animal that hunts other animals for food.
prey
An animal that is hunted by another animal.
survive
To stay alive in spite of dangers.
reproduce
To have young, so there are new living things of the same kind.
