Glossary

nectar
A sweet juice that flowers keep inside. Many animals drink it as food.
pollen
A very fine dust, usually yellow, that flowers make. A flower needs it to make seeds.
pollinate
To carry pollen from one flower to another flower. Bees, butterflies, and hummingbirds do it.
fruit
The part of a plant that grows from the flower and holds the seeds. An apple and an acorn are fruits.
seed
A small part of a plant that can grow into a new plant.
disperse
To spread seeds far from the parent plant, so they can grow in other places.
