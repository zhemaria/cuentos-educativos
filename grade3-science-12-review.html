Glossary

force
A push or a pull that can move an object, stop it or change its direction.
magnet
An object that attracts iron without touching it. Its like poles repel and its opposite poles attract.
metamorphosis
A big change in body shape during an animal's life, like from caterpillar to butterfly.
inherited trait
A characteristic passed from parents to their young, like eye color.
fossil
The remains or marks of a living thing from long ago, saved in rock.
camouflage
Colors or shapes that help a living thing blend in with the place where it lives.
climate
The usual weather of a place over many years.
