The cup phone

The story

Adrián and Israel wanted to talk from far away without shouting.

They made a phone with two paper cups and a long string.

Adrián talked into his cup: “Can you hear me, Israel?”

His voice made the string vibrate, and the sound traveled to the other cup.

“I hear you!” Israel answered, with the cup on his ear.

But when the string was loose, they could not hear anything.

“We have to pull the string tight!” said Adrián.

Later, at night, they made up another signal.

With a flashlight, one flash meant “hello,” and two flashes meant “come and play.”

Israel saw two flashes in the window and ran out to play.

Adrián brought his green dinosaur so it could play too.

With sound or with light, we can send a message to someone far away.
