The story

For a school assignment, Lucía had to find out the of her neighborhood: how it has changed over time. She asked Grandpa for help, since he has lived there for fifty years. Grandpa took out a box full of old photos.

"These photos are from the ," he said, "the time that has already gone by." One photo showed an empty lot full of trash. "That is where the garden is now!" Lucía exclaimed.

Many years ago, the neighbors cleaned up that lot and planted the first plants. It was a very good for the neighborhood. A change is when something becomes different from how it was before.

Another photo showed an old brick factory. "That factory closed," Grandpa said, "and years later they turned it into the neighborhood library." Now children go there to read and use the computers.

Grandpa also told her that, when he arrived, the sidewalks had no ramps at the corners. "People in wheelchairs, like Mrs. Elena, could hardly cross the street," he said. "The ramps changed their lives: now they can go to many places on their own."

Over the years, families from many countries came to the neighborhood. They opened stores, restaurants, and bakeries, like Mr. Julio's. The neighborhood filled with new languages, music, and flavors.

Lucía put all the changes in order on a , a line where events are placed in the order they happened. At the end, she drew the , the time right now: the neighborhood with its garden, its library, and its ramps.

"And what will you put in the ?" Grandpa asked. "A new park with swings for everyone," Lucía answered. "I want to change my neighborhood for the better too!" The future is the time that has not come yet.
