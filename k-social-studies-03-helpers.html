The story

Every morning, Adrián and Génesis walk to school.

On the corner is Mr. Pablo, the crossing guard. He holds up his stop sign, and the cars stop.

"Thank you, Mr. Pablo!" the children say, and they cross safely.

At the door, Mrs. Morales is waiting. She is the school principal.

She makes sure the school is a safe place where everyone can learn.

Today there is a special visit: a red truck comes to the school!

A firefighter climbs down with her helmet. Her name is Carmen.

"My job is to put out fires and help people who are in danger," she says.

Adrián tries on the helmet. It is way too big!

Everyone has a job, and together we take care of our community.
