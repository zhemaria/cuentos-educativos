The story

At recess, the soccer field was a mess. The older kids always kept the ball, and some third graders ended up fighting and crying.

Back in the classroom, Mrs. Kim had everyone sit in a circle. "We have a problem," she said. "What can we do?" "We need !" said Israel. "A rule tells what we should or shouldn't do in a group."

Everyone shared ideas. Mei suggested taking turns on the field. Jamal suggested mixed teams. Lucía asked that nobody be left out of the game.

"A good rule has to be ," the teacher explained. "It has to treat people with respect and consider what they need to participate." Together they talked about each idea and then voted. Three rules won, and they wrote them on a poster.

"Rules give us ," said Mrs. Kim, "things we need to do, like taking turns and taking care of the ball." And they also protect our and our : everyone has the right to play.

That afternoon, Israel told his dad all about it in the car. When they came to a red light, his dad stopped. "The community rules that the government makes are called ," he said. "This law protects us and the people crossing the street."

"What if someone doesn't follow a law?" asked Israel. "Then there's a , like paying a fine. Consequences help everyone follow the rules."

The next day, recess was different. The teams took turns, everyone played and nobody cried. Even the older kids said it was more fun that way.

"Rules aren't there to take away the fun," Israel thought. "They're there so everyone can enjoy it."

Glossary

rule
What we should or shouldn't do in a group, like a family, a class or a game.
law
A rule made by the government that everyone in a place must follow.
responsibility
Something we need to do, like following rules or taking care of things we share.
right
Something every person deserves and is allowed to do, like learning or playing.
freedom
Being able to choose and act without being unfairly stopped.
fair
Treating people with respect and considering what they need to participate.
consequence
What happens as a result of what we do, for example, for breaking a rule.
