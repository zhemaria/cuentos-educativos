The story

On Saturdays, many neighbors work in the neighborhood garden. But Mrs. Elena, a neighbor who uses a wheelchair, could only watch from the sidewalk. There were three steps to get into the garden.

"It is not fair that Mrs. Elena cannot come in," said Lucía. Grandpa agreed and called the neighbors to a . A meeting is when a group of people get together to talk and decide something.

At the meeting, everyone shared ideas. Mrs. Elena shared her ideas, too. "A ramp would help me enter, and a raised planter would let me garden comfortably." Mrs. Elena and her neighbors decided to build a ramp next to the steps and a raised planter box, so she could plant without bending down. "If we all , we will finish in one day," said Grandpa. Then they shared out the . A role is the job each person has in a group. Grandpa and other adults would build the wooden ramp. Israel and his mom would fill the planter box with soil. Lucía would paint a welcome sign.

Each person had a , something they had to do well because the others were counting on them. Other came too: people who help without being paid.

The next Saturday, everyone worked from early in the morning. There were saws, nails, soil, and paint everywhere. By lunchtime, the ramp and the planter box were ready.

Mrs. Elena rolled up the ramp in her wheelchair and came into the garden for the first time. She planted tomatoes in the raised planter box. "Thank you, neighbors," she said, her eyes shining.

"Now the garden is ," said Grandpa. "Everyone can use it, because each person has what they need to take part." Lucía hung her sign at the entrance: "A garden for everyone."
