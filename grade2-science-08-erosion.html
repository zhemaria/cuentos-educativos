The story

In the neighborhood garden, there is a small hill. After a strong storm, Lucía saw that soil from the hill had slid down onto the path. "Grandpa, the hill is falling apart!" she said.

"Rainwater runs down the and carries away the ," Grandpa explained. A slope is land that tilts up or down, and soil is the layer of earth where plants grow. "This is ," he added. "It happens when water, wind, or ice carries soil or rock away, sometimes slowly and sometimes quickly. This storm did it very fast!"

Israel came with his mom to help. Together they looked for a , a way to solve the problem. They had two ideas, and they decided to test them on different parts of the hill.

On the first part, Lucía planted grass and little plants. Their grow under the ground and hold the soil, like fingers.

On the second part, Israel and Grandpa built a low of stones. The wall would stop the water and soil before they reached the path. On the third part, they did nothing. "That way we can compare," said Grandpa. "We will know if the solutions really work."

A few weeks went by, and the grass grew. Then another storm came, and the next day they went back to the garden to observe the results.

The part with nothing had lost a lot of soil. The part with the wall had lost a little, because water went over some of the stones. The part with plants had lost almost no soil, because the roots held it very well.

"Both solutions help," said Lucía, "but the plants worked best." Since that day, the whole hill in the garden has been covered with grass and flowers.

Glossary

slope
Land that tilts up or down, like the side of a hill.
soil
The layer of earth that covers the ground, where plants grow.
erosion
When water, wind, or ice carries soil or rock away. It can happen slowly or quickly.
solution
A way to solve a problem.
root
The part of a plant that grows under the ground. It holds the plant and also holds the soil.
wall
A wall, sometimes low, made of stones, bricks, or other materials.
